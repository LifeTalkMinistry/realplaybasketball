REAL PLAY BASKETBALL

LESS SCREEN. REAL POINTS.
COME PLAY.

Official website project for Real Play Basketball and the Real Play Community Fund.

Real Play Basketball is a community basketball movement that creates more opportunities for kids, young people, and adults to physically play basketball, move, compete, connect, and experience real-world play.

Real Play is pro-play, not anti-screen. The goal is not to shame technology, gaming, social media, or digital life. The goal is simply to make sure real-world physical play still has a place.

1. Website Purpose

This website is the official public home of Real Play Basketball.

It must allow anyone to quickly understand:

What Real Play is

Why Real Play exists

How to join and play

How to support the community program

How much support has been received

How much money has already been put into play

Exactly where that money went

What real-world impact was created

The website must work cleanly on:

Mobile phones

Tablets

Laptops

Desktop browsers

Mobile is not a reduced version of the website. The same mission, transparency, and primary actions must remain clear on every screen size.

5. Transparency Is a Core Feature

The website must not merely say that Real Play is transparent.

It should show the transparency publicly.

The financial transparency system must distinguish at minimum:

Total Support Received

All confirmed money that entered the Real Play Community Fund.

Put Into Play

Money that has actually been used toward Real Play activities, equipment, courts, projects, or approved program costs.

Available for Play

Confirmed fund balance that has not yet been used.

Basic relationship:

AVAILABLE FOR PLAY = TOTAL SUPPORT RECEIVED - PUT INTO PLAY

Example:

REAL PLAY FUND

₱102,350
TOTAL SUPPORT RECEIVED

₱81,420
PUT INTO PLAY

₱20,930
AVAILABLE FOR PLAY

The phrase PUT INTO PLAY is preferred over generic wording such as “money invested” because it is tied directly to the Real Play brand and does not imply a financial investment product.

6. No Fake “Live” Numbers

The public amount must never be a manually animated marketing number pretending to be live.

If the site says an amount is live or current, it should come from the real public ledger/data source.

Do not create fake:

Donations

Expenses

Projects

Receipts

Player counts

Impact numbers

Development/demo data must be clearly marked as demo data.

21. Decision Filter

Before approving any website feature, ask:

Mission Test

Does this help people understand, join, support, or trust Real Play?

Basketball Test

Is basketball still central?

Transparency Test

If this involves money or impact, is it based on real recorded data?

Brand Test

Does it feel like an invitation to play rather than a lecture about screens?

Simplicity Test

Are we making Real Play easier to understand or unnecessarily complicated?
